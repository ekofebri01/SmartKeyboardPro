Smart Keyboard Pro is a fast, lightweight, and modern open-source Android virtual keyboard built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Key Features:
- Lightning-Fast & Lightweight: Optimized codebase with a minimal APK footprint and zero background drain.
- Smart Auto-Text & Shortcuts: Expand frequently used abbreviations and canned responses instantly.
- Local Predictive Text & Dictionary: Offline word prediction and learning supporting Indonesian and English dictionaries.
- Customizable Themes & Key Dimensions: Choose from classic themes or build your own custom color palette and key heights.
- Universal Backup & Restore: Export and import your settings, custom dictionary words, and auto-text shortcuts via JSON or ZIP files.
- Privacy First: 100% offline-capable with no telemetry, tracking, or network permissions required for core typing.
- Open Source: Free software released under the GNU General Public License v3 (GPL-3.0).
