Smart Keyboard Pro is a lightweight, customizable, and completely private keyboard application for Android.

Key Features:
- 100% Offline & Private: No internet permission declared or used. Your typing never leaves your device.
- Full layout support: QWERTY, numeric, and symbol keyboards.
- Built-in Emoji picker for quick expressions.
- Haptic vibration and sound feedback on keypresses.
- Modern Material 3 UI with dark mode support.
- Fully compatible with F-Droid and open-source standards.
